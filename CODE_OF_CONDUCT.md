# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free
for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent;
- focus criticism on ideas and code;
- protect other users' privacy and credentials;
- accept maintainers' moderation decisions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information and
deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
violates these expectations.
